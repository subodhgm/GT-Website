Operating model

A delivery model that
survives contact with reality.

Five phases, one continuous thread of engineering evidence. Strategy sets the constraint, architecture makes it real, and operations keep it true.

  1. 01

    Discover

    Constraint mapping across systems, data, regulation and the commercial model.

    Output Opportunity map & value model

  2. 02

    Define

    Target architecture, delivery sequencing and the metric each release must move.

    Output Architecture & measurable roadmap

  3. 03

    Design

    Product experience, service blueprints and the interfaces between teams as well as systems.

    Output Validated experience & system design

  4. 04

    Deliver

    Embedded product teams shipping in two-week increments behind automated quality gates.

    Output Production releases & adoption telemetry

  5. 05

    Evolve

    Reliability engineering, cost control and continuous capability transfer to your teams.

    Output Service levels & internal capability
